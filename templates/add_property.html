ADMIN CONTROL   Guest Capacity & Pricing

You do not need to enter maximum guests or pricing.

These settings are controlled exclusively by the Goinn Admin team.

  • Your property will first be submitted as Pending.
  • The Admin will decide the Maximum Number of Guests.
  • The Admin will configure Individual Pricing or Group Pricing.
  • The property will only become visible to customers after Admin approval.
⏳
What happens after submission?
Your property will appear in the Admin Dashboard with a Pending status. The Admin will review the property, set the maximum guest capacity and configure the pricing before approving it.
